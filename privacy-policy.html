1. Scope and audience

This Privacy Policy describes how the Company handles information in connection with CPA, a private internal accounting tool that helps an authorized Owner retrieve QuickBooks Online financial/accounting information and approve proposed bookkeeping entries with assistance from Grok Bot (through the Cursor / Grok Bot service).

CPA is intended for authorized internal business use by the Company on devices the Company controls (for example, an authorized phone or laptop) in a QuickBooks Online–licensed environment. It is not marketed to the general public, is not intended as a consumer app store product, and is not designed for advertising or lead generation.

This Policy applies to the Company’s operation of CPA and to these public legal pages. It does not replace Intuit’s, Cursor’s, Grok Bot’s, email providers’, or other third parties’ own privacy terms for their platforms.

2. What CPA is

CPA is designed to:

  • Connect an authorized Owner’s QuickBooks Online company to Grok Bot using a local connector process;
  • Retrieve accounting-related data needed for bookkeeping review (for example, account lists, expenses, vendors, and card/purchase-related transactions available through the QuickBooks Online API);
  • Allow review of receipt-related email content the Owner makes available for expense categorization;
  • Propose bookkeeping entries for human review; and
  • Create or change QuickBooks Online entries only after the authorized user approves them.

CPA is not intended as a system of record for veterinary patient charts, clinical notes, regulated animal-health records, payroll tax filings, or consumer marketing profiles. QuickBooks Online and email systems remain the underlying systems of record for accounting and message data they store.

3. Where CPA runs (local vs public pages)

Current development status: The CPA application and QuickBooks connector are hosted and run locally on the Owner’s computer (a local process / MCP-style connector), not as a publicly hosted multi-tenant accounting website operated by the Company.

OAuth redirect configuration: The connector’s configured redirect URI for interactive authorization uses a localhost callback (for example, http://localhost:8000/callback) during local development / sandbox-style setup. The Company does not intend to operate a publicly hosted CPA connector that serves QuickBooks data to the open internet.

Accuracy note for Intuit production apps: Intuit’s developer requirements for production QuickBooks Online apps may require a publicly reachable HTTPS redirect URI for the initial OAuth authorization handshake even when day-to-day API use continues from a local connector after tokens are obtained. Any production authorization arrangement must follow Intuit’s then-current requirements. That temporary callback need (if required) is not the same as publishing CPA or QuickBooks credentials as a public service. Sandbox/development commonly allows localhost redirects; production rules may differ.

These legal pages: If published on GitHub Pages, only the static legal HTML/CSS files are intended to be public. This legal site does not include application logs, analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or QuickBooks data.

4. Categories of data

Depending on how the Owner uses CPA, the following categories may be processed:

4.1 QuickBooks Online accounting data

Retrieved through the QuickBooks Online API using the Company’s authorized connection, which may include:

  • Chart of accounts / account lists and related metadata;
  • Expense, bill, purchase, vendor, and related transaction information;
  • Card / purchase activity reflected in QuickBooks Online;
  • Vendors and payees;
  • Company information and other accounting objects the connected app permissions allow; and
  • Entries created, updated, or voided only after user approval when write tools are enabled and used.

The underlying connector library can expose a broad QuickBooks Online tool surface (read/search and optional create/update/delete categories). Company practice for CPA is to use the connection for internal bookkeeping review and to require user approval before writing entries. Broader entity types that may exist in the library (for example, customers, invoices, or employees) are not the intended focus of CPA’s expense-review workflow and should not be treated as a promise that CPA is a payroll, tax-preparation, CRM, or clinical records product.

4.2 Receipt and email content

When the Owner uses CPA with receipt emails, message content, attachments, merchant names, amounts, dates, and related expense clues may be reviewed to propose categorizations. Email remains stored with the Owner’s email provider unless the Owner separately saves copies elsewhere.

4.3 Operational / conversation content with Grok Bot

Prompts, tool results, approvals, and related operational content may be processed by the Grok Bot / Cursor service so the assistant can help retrieve data and draft proposed entries. That processing is subject to those services’ terms and privacy practices.

4.4 Authentication secrets

QuickBooks Online OAuth client credentials, access tokens, refresh tokens, and realm/company identifiers are used to call Intuit APIs. See Credentials and token handling.

4.5 Data we do not intentionally collect via these legal pages

The static GitHub Pages legal site is not designed to collect visitor accounts, payment cards, precise geolocation, advertising identifiers, or analytics profiles.

5. How data is obtained

  • QuickBooks Online API over HTTPS to Intuit endpoints (sandbox or production, depending on configuration), after OAuth 2.0 authorization;
  • Email / receipt sources the Owner chooses to open or provide for review;
  • User input in Grok Bot / Cursor conversations (approvals, corrections, instructions); and
  • Local configuration on the Owner’s device (for example, environment variables or a local token store file used by the connector).

6. How data is used

We use the information described above to:

  • Help the authorized Owner review expenses and related accounting activity;
  • Propose bookkeeping categorizations and entries for human approval;
  • Create or update QuickBooks Online records only after approval;
  • Maintain the QuickBooks Online connection (including token refresh); and
  • Respond to privacy or access-control requests related to CPA.

We do not use CPA data to sell advertising or to build third-party marketing profiles.

7. Credentials and token handling

Intuit / QuickBooks Online credentials and OAuth tokens for CPA are intended to be handled in a secure local environment on authorized Company devices. In the current connector design:

  • Client ID, client secret, refresh token, and realm ID are read from a local configuration / token store (commonly a local .env file or an absolute path configured for token storage);
  • Token refresh is performed using the Intuit OAuth library (intuit-oauth), which obtains new access tokens and may rotate refresh tokens;
  • Rotated tokens are written back to the local token store by the connector process; and
  • The Company does not operate a separate permanent external database of QuickBooks secrets for CPA apart from the platforms the Owner already uses (QuickBooks Online / Intuit, email, and the Grok Bot / Cursor service environment).

Access to those devices and secrets should be limited to authorized users. Compromised device access can expose local tokens; revoke the QuickBooks connection promptly if a device is lost or credentials may be exposed.

8. Grok Bot, Cursor, and other services

CPA relies on third-party services. Those providers may process operational and content data (including prompts, tool outputs, and related metadata) according to their terms, privacy policies, and subprocessors:

  • Intuit / QuickBooks Online — accounting system of record and API platform;
  • Grok Bot / Cursor service — assistant runtime that may process conversation and tool data needed to operate CPA;
  • Email provider(s) — if receipt emails are reviewed; and
  • GitHub Pages — optional public hosting for these static legal pages only.

The Company does not control every subprocessors list or retention default of those providers. Review their current documentation for details. Encryption, access controls, and retention on those platforms are subject to each provider’s capabilities and policies; this Policy does not claim absolute, Company-controlled encryption of all copies everywhere those services store data.

9. Sharing, sale, and advertising

  • We do not sell CPA-related personal or accounting data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising tracking pixels or ad networks on these legal pages.
  • We share data with service providers only as needed to operate CPA (Intuit, Grok Bot / Cursor, email, and similar infrastructure the Owner configures).
  • We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

10. Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the system that holds the data:

  • QuickBooks Online — retained according to Intuit / Company QuickBooks settings and accounting needs;
  • Source email — retained according to the email provider and the Owner’s mailbox practices;
  • Grok Bot / Cursor service — retained according to that service’s retention practices;
  • Local token store / device files — retained on the Owner’s device until deleted, rotated, or revoked; and
  • User requests — we will consider deletion or restriction requests regarding Company-controlled CPA materials, subject to legitimate legal, security, audit, tax, and backup needs.

We do not make unsupported promises of instantaneous or absolute deletion of every copy across third-party platforms, backups, or legally required records.

11. Security measures (qualified)

Reasonable safeguards the Company intends to use include:

  • Limiting CPA and QuickBooks credential access to authorized Owner devices;
  • Keeping the connector local rather than publishing it as an open accounting API;
  • Using OAuth 2.0 tokens instead of embedding user passwords in CPA; and
  • Relying on HTTPS for QuickBooks Online API calls as provided by Intuit’s endpoints.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Provider-side encryption and controls apply only to the extent those providers implement them.

12. Privacy requests and revocation

Revoking QuickBooks access

  • Disconnect / revoke the app in Intuit’s connected apps / QuickBooks Online company authorization settings; and/or
  • Remove local CPA connector credentials/token files and stop running the local connector on Company devices (GitHub Pages legal hosting is independent of QuickBooks access control).

Privacy or data requests

Email info@mapleridgemobilevet.com with “CPA Privacy Request” in the subject line. We may need to verify that the requester is an authorized Company contact. Responses may be limited for data held only by Intuit, email providers, or the Grok Bot / Cursor service; we can help identify where to direct those platform requests when appropriate.

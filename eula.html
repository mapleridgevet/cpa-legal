4. Description of CPA

CPA is an internal tool that enables an authorized Owner to connect QuickBooks Online to Grok Bot (via the Cursor / Grok Bot service) to retrieve financial/accounting transactions and to approve proposed entries. In the current development configuration, CPA and its QuickBooks connector run locally on the Owner’s PC as a local process; they are not published from the Company’s GitHub Pages legal site as a publicly hosted connector.

Interactive OAuth setup may use a localhost redirect URI during development/sandbox use. Production QuickBooks Online app authorization may be subject to Intuit’s then-current redirect-URI requirements (which can differ from sandbox localhost rules) without converting CPA into a publicly hosted accounting data service. See the Privacy Policy hosting section.

Bookkeeping entries are intended to be created or changed in QuickBooks Online only after explicit user approval when write capabilities are used.
